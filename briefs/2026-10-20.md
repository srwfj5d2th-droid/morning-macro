## Concept of the day — Unit 6 — Positioning & sentiment vs. fundamentals, segment 4

**A rally can be just as narrow as a selloff.** Segments 1 through 3 used breadth to read selloffs: a cap-weighted index falling harder than the equal-weight average says the selling was concentrated in a few giant stocks, not broad-based.
