## Concept of the day — Unit 8 — Cross-asset confirmation, segment 7 reinforcement (R)

**Same assets falling, different reason — gold and copper's drop wasn't the dollar's doing this time.** Segment 7 taught that gold and copper are dollar-priced, so when both fall together it's often the dollar doing the work, not an independent vote on growth or fear — Thursday's brief used exactly that mechanism to explain gold's drop. Today looks the same on the surface: gold fell 4.62% and copper fell 2.63%, both flagged for a third straight session. But the mechanism isn't the same. The dollar barely moved Friday (DXY −0.09%, essentially flat) while Treasurys rallied and credit stayed wide — the textbook flight-to-safety signature. Gold, the asset that's supposed to benefit most from exactly that signature, fell instead.

The habit worth building: check the mechanism fresh each session rather than assuming yesterday's explanation still applies because the price action looks similar. With the dollar not doing the work Friday, the honest read is a broader liquidation that didn't spare gold or copper either — not a repeat of last week's dollar-driven story, even though the two charts look alike.
