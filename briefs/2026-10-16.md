## Concept of the day — Unit 6 — Positioning & sentiment vs. fundamentals, segment 2

**Rotation or retreat? Two days, two breadth readings.** Segment 1 introduced breadth: the index versus the average stock. Thursday is Wednesday’s mirror image. On Wednesday SPY (cap-weighted) fell 1.08% and RSP (equal-weight) fell 0.57%. On Thursday SPY rose 0.28% and RSP rose 1.37%. Over the two days, SPY is down 0.80% and RSP is up 0.80%. That is what a rotation looks like: money moving between groups of stocks (out of the biggest AI names, into software, homebuilders and health care), not money leaving stocks. A retreat looks different: most stocks fall together, and the worry shows up in credit. Here is the complication. Credit did react: junk-bond spreads widened 9bp on Wednesday’s selloff day (CL-0043, confirmed). So the honest reading is mostly rotation, with a credit warning attached, and the warning sits where the debt is: AI builders that borrowed heavily. Unit 6’s question applies too. A rotation changes which companies investors favor. It doesn’t, by itself, say the economy changed.

**Client translation:** "The market didn’t really go up or down this week so much as shuffle: money left the most popular AI stocks and spread into everything else, which is why an evenly spread portfolio held up better than the headline index."
